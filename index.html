    FAQ

    Questions,
    answered.

    Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out and we'll reply within one business day.

    Which model should I start with?

    It depends on your capital and goals. Dropshipping has the lowest entry cost, wholesale is the fastest route to proven sales, and white label builds the most valuable long-term brand. We'll recommend one on your strategy call.

    Do you manage accounts I already own?

    Yes. We can take over and optimise existing Amazon, eBay, Etsy, TikTok Shop, Shopify and Walmart accounts, or open and verify new ones for you.

    How does your cold calling service work?

    We research your ideal customers, build a script together, and our SDRs call decision makers on your behalf. Qualified prospects are booked into your calendar, and you get recordings and weekly reports.

    How soon will I see results?

    Timelines vary by model: dropshipping and wholesale can produce first sales within weeks, white label usually needs a few months for launch and ranking. Outbound campaigns typically deliver first meetings in the first couple of weeks.

    Is there a long-term contract?

    We keep engagements flexible. Most clients start with a short initial term so you can see our work before committing further.

    Do you work with clients worldwide?

    Yes. We support sellers and businesses across all major regions and time zones.
