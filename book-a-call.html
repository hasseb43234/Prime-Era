Before you book

Good to
know.

Is the call really free?

Yes. The call is free and carries no obligation. If we're a good fit we'll explain how we could work together; if not, we'll say so.

How long does it take?

Thirty minutes. Booking takes about a minute — choose a topic and time, then add your details.

Who will I speak with?

A member of the Prime Era team who works on the service you're asking about, not a generic call handler.

What should I prepare?

Nothing is required. If you have a store link, a product idea or a target market, mention it in the notes and we'll come prepared.

Can I change the time?

Of course. Reply to the confirmation email and we'll move it to another slot.
